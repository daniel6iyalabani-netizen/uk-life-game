UK LIFE 🏙️

Explore a UK-inspired 3D city at your own pace: take on 28 starter missions, discover hidden destinations, try dozens of careers, unlock cars, customise your look, adopt a pet and build your home.

No rush and no real-money purchases — play your way, save your progress, and come back whenever you feel like exploring.

You start on foot with £250. Buy your first car from the dealership after earning or saving enough.

WASD move/drive · E enter/exit car or building · F interact/board bus · Q choose nearby car · C camera · Space handbrake · Shift sprint · Mouse look. Walk to a door and press E to enter.

Prototype for Chrome. The 3D engine loads from the internet. Building interiors are compact explorable rooms, and NPCs use simple walking routes.
