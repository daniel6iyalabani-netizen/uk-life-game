🏙️ CITY BOTS

A first-person 3D-style city training game built for Chromebooks. Find the friendly training bots around the streets and tag their glowing targets. No downloads or 3D graphics acceleration needed.

Computer: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to tag, Shift to sprint, R to restart.
Touchscreen: use the direction pad and TAG BOT button.

Lightweight canvas renderer — works without WebGL.
